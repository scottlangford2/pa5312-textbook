# Program Evaluation and Administrative Statistical Analysis

**PA 5312 — Texas State University, Master of Public Administration**
*Dr. W. Scott Langford, Assistant Professor, Department of Political Science*

This is the online companion to the PA 5312 graduate seminar. It teaches program evaluation as a practical, decision-oriented craft: how to ask whether a public or nonprofit program actually works, how to estimate its effects credibly, and how to communicate the evidence honestly to the people who make decisions. Analysis is done in **Microsoft Excel**, building on the statistical foundation from PA 5311.
